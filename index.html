iGloo App AI Review: Build Client-Ready Communities With AI

If you are looking for a way to build online communities for your audience or clients, iGloo App AI takes a different approach.

Instead of treating AI as a simple writing assistant, iGloo is designed to let a connected AI tool help assemble parts of a real community inside the platform.

The idea is straightforward: provide a brief, connect a compatible AI such as Claude or ChatGPT, let the AI help create the first version, then review and customize the result before publishing.

What Is iGloo App AI?

iGloo is a community platform that brings several parts of the member experience together, including:

  • Community posts and discussions
  • Courses, modules and lessons
  • Events
  • Members and chat
  • Joining questions
  • Rewards and gamification
  • Landing and welcome experiences

The platform also supports AI-assisted workflows through compatible Claude, ChatGPT or MCP connections.

The important point is that the AI is not presented as a replacement for the person building the community.

You provide the brief, review the generated content, replace placeholders, apply your branding and decide what gets published.

How The AI-Assisted Workflow Works

The basic workflow can be thought of as:

Brief → AI-assisted build → Review → Customize → Publish

For example, a business could need a community for customer onboarding, education, events or member engagement.

Instead of starting every section from scratch, the connected AI can help assemble supported elements such as communities, posts, courses, lessons, events, joining questions and rewards.

You still control the final result.

That distinction is important because iGloo does not claim that AI can independently run an entire community business with one click.

Building Communities For Clients

One of the more interesting aspects of iGloo is its Commercial Rights option.

The Commercial Rights offer is designed for people who want to create communities for clients.

According to the current offer, it costs $37 as a one-time payment and allows unlimited client communities, with up to 250 members in each community.

The iGloo branding remains visible with Commercial Rights.

For agencies looking for a white-label client presentation, iGloo offers a separate Agency Suite option.

This makes the licensing differences important to understand before purchasing.

Personal vs Commercial Rights

The Personal option is intended for people building communities for their own use.

It is listed at $27 one time and supports up to three communities, with up to 250 members per community.

Commercial Rights is listed at $37 one time and is intended for client and commercial use, with unlimited communities and up to 250 members per community.

So the appropriate option depends largely on what you plan to build.

If you are creating a community for your own audience, Personal may be sufficient.

If you are planning to offer community setup as a service to clients, Commercial Rights is the option specifically positioned for that use case.

What You Should Know About The AI

There is an important distinction between the core AI workflow and the separate Command Center product.

For the core workflow, you need a compatible Claude, ChatGPT or MCP connection. The AI service itself is not included with the iGloo purchase.

Command Center is a separate iGloo-run AI offer. It includes a finite amount of prepaid credits, with optional one-time credit purchases if additional credits are needed.

So it would be inaccurate to describe iGloo as including unlimited AI.

Things To Consider Before Buying

You also still need to provide the strategy and review the AI-generated work.

Commercial Rights also does not mean white-label. iGloo branding remains visible unless you choose an option that provides the relevant presentation controls.

Final Thoughts

The main idea behind iGloo App AI is not simply generating text with AI.

The platform is designed to help turn a community brief into a working first version inside an actual community platform.

For someone building communities for their own audience, the Personal option provides a straightforward entry point.

For freelancers and agencies interested in creating communities for clients, Commercial Rights is the more relevant option because it is specifically positioned around client work and unlimited community creation within its stated member limits.

If you are considering iGloo, review the current plans, features and licensing terms before making a decision.
