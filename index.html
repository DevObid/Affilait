A Few Useful Answers

Before you join.

Need help with registration? Contact iGloo support →

Can I use the Lead Finder right now?
Register here, then use the account-access link to create or open your verified iGloo account with the same email. The working Lead Finder is inside iGloo — the interface shown on this page is only a preview.
Does registering automatically create my account?
No. This form saves your launch registration and sends a webinar confirmation. The next screen gives you a separate secure link to create or open your iGloo account. Use the same email for both.
Do I need to buy anything or attend live to receive the gift?
No. The Local Lead Finder is included with registration after you verify your iGloo account. Paid iGloo plans and upgrades are optional and will be explained during the webinar.
What contact information will the tool provide?
The tool will show business websites and phone numbers when they are publicly listed. Details vary by business. It will not guarantee email addresses, contact names, or guarantee clients.
Is this the same as the paid Client Finder Pack?
No. The free Local Lead Finder is for discovering potential businesses to approach. The paid Client Finder Pack is the separate client pipeline, research workflow and delivery offer shown in the launch options above.
Is iGloo a subscription?
No. Every product in the offer stack, including the Complete Bundle, is a one-time payment.
What is Commercial Rights?
Commercial Rights adds client and commercial-use rights with unlimited communities (up to 250 members per community) and iGloo branding. It is not the Agency Suite.
What does PRO add?
iGloo PRO adds a white-label logo, custom domain, premium styling, and growth, retention and monetisation training. It does not increase the community allowance attached to your license.
What is Command Center?
Command Center is iGloo-run AI powered by a finite prepaid credit pack, with optional one-time credit rebuys. There is no automatic recurring rebill, and it is not unlimited AI or unlimited credits.
What is Agency Suite?
Agency Suite includes white-label client presentation, agency resources, and an unlimited portfolio. It does not include Command Center credits by itself.
When is the live launch?
Sunday 13 September · 3 PM ET. Register for the viewing link and reminders. The free Lead Finder is available after verified account setup whether or not you attend live.
Does iGloo guarantee clients or sales?
No. iGloo is software and does not guarantee clients, sales, income, or business results.
